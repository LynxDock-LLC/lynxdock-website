LYNXDOCK 0.3.0 BETA PARTICIPATION REQUIREMENTS
Version 1.0
Effective date: 2026-11-05

Issued by LynxDock LLC ("LynxDock"). Questions: admin@lynxdock.app.

1. WHO THESE REQUIREMENTS ARE FOR
These requirements apply to you if you take part in a LynxDock 0.3.0
beta community using an earlier LynxDock app build that shows no licence
screen (for example the 0.2.6 app), however you were invited: directly
by a community's operator, or after requesting access on LynxDock's
website.
   If you use a 0.3.0 beta build, you accept the LynxDock Beta
Evaluation Licence (LYNXDOCK-BETA-EVAL-1.0) in that build instead, and
these requirements do not apply to that build.

2. WHAT YOUR ACKNOWLEDGEMENT DOES AND DOES NOT DO
Your acknowledgement concerns your taking part in the beta only. It does
not license, replace or change the licence or terms that govern your
earlier build, and it does not grant or withdraw any rights you already
have in that build. It does not bring your earlier build under the Beta
Evaluation Licence, does not mean you accepted that licence, and does
not apply that licence's terms - its liability cap included - to your
earlier build. If you later install a build covered by the Beta
Evaluation Licence, you accept that licence for that build when it first
starts.

3. ELIGIBILITY: 18 OR OLDER
You must be at least 18 years old and able to enter a binding agreement.
You declare this by ticking the declaration on LynxDock's tester page at
https://lynxdock.app/beta/. This is an affirmative self-declaration, not
age verification. LynxDock does not independently verify your age and
asks for no date of birth or proof of age. If LynxDock learns that an
acknowledgement came from someone under 18, it deletes that
acknowledgement and asks the community not to invite them.

4. WHEN AND HOW TO ACKNOWLEDGE
First, before the community's operator invites you, read these
requirements. Then, on the tester page, tick the box declaring that you
are 18 or older and the box acknowledging these requirements, and send
the form. It sends your name, email address and community, with both
declarations, to the site's form service (Formspree). It sends nothing
to the community's server or its operator. Finally, tell the operator
who is inviting you that you have acknowledged the requirements.
LynxDock does not confirm acknowledgements to community operators; the
operator relies on what you tell them.

6. YOUR INFORMATION
When you acknowledge, the tester page sends your name, email address,
community, your 18+ declaration and your acknowledgement to Formspree,
the site's form service, which emails a notification to LynxDock's
monitored mailbox. LynxDock keeps your acknowledgement, as that email
notification, while your participation is active and afterwards for one
year. Each submission also says which form it came from and the page it
was sent from. The LynxDock website privacy notice
(https://lynxdock.app/privacy/) gives the details.
